…or paste notes
What formats work?
  • term: definition, term - definition, term :: definition
  • Q: question followed by A: answer
  • CSV / TSV with two columns (question, answer)
  • JSON: [{"q":"…","a":"…"}]
  • Anything else: sentences become fill-in-the-blank cards

PDFs work too (they need selectable text, not scanned pictures). For Word docs, copy the text and paste it above.
